# Code of Conduct

<p align="center"><img src="docs/media/scenes/hands.webp" alt="Two characters walking together hand in hand at dusk" width="560" /></p>

I use the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as the code of conduct here. In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; many people here are new to open
  source, 3D or the web.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people. No harassment, insults, sexual
  content or unwelcome attention, and no publishing anyone's private
  information.
- Remember what this is for: people use it to make something personal
  for someone they care about. Never ask anyone to share their photos,
  letters or personal details, and never post other people's.

These rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else
you're representing this project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through
GitHub's private reporting for this repository (**Security → Report a
vulnerability**, starting the title with "Conduct:"), or use GitHub's
**Report content** on the comment or issue. I'm the only one who reads these
reports and I keep them confidential. I may edit or remove content and warn,
suspend or ban people, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
