## Characters: MakeHuman / MPFB (CC0)

The two characters are built with [MPFB](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html)
(MakeHuman for Blender) from the **MakeHuman system assets** (base body,
skins, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, hair `braid01` and `short04`, clothes
`male_worksuit01`, `male_casualsuit01`, `shoes01`, `shoes03`) and the
**faceunits01** ARKit face targets, all released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by the MakeHuman
team (the asset files state "explicitly released as CC0"). Clothes were
recoloured. MPFB itself (GPL) is an authoring tool and is not included.

## Adding assets

New assets must be CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA (or your own work
released under the project's licence), with the source, author and licence
added to this file in the same change. No assets with "non-commercial",
"no derivatives" or "no redistribution" terms, nothing from Mixamo or store
packs that forbid stand-alone redistribution, and no photos of real people.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#assets).
